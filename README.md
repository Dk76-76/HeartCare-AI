**CardioAI** is a web-based machine learning application designed to evaluate diagnostic parameters and predict the probability of cardiovascular disease. By analyzing standard clinical and demographic data, the application generates a comprehensive risk assessment report to assist in preliminary health evaluations.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Real-time AI Assessment:** Runs inference via advanced neural models to predict heart disease risk probability instantly.
*   **Comprehensive Clinical Reports:** Automatically generates detailed, printable assessment reports including patient information, clinical parameters, and AI diagnostic scoring.
*   **Secure & Private:** Data privacy is prioritized. Predictions are generated securely, ensuring no sensitive patient data is transmitted or stored externally.
*   **Responsive UI/UX:** A clean, professional, and intuitive interface designed for seamless use by both healthcare professionals and individuals.

---

## 📊 Clinical Parameters Evaluated

The model assesses cardiovascular risk based on the following key metrics:
*   **Demographics:** Age, Sex
*   **Vital Signs:** Resting Blood Pressure, Max Heart Rate (Achieved)
*   **Blood Work:** Serum Cholesterol, Fasting Blood Sugar
*   **Cardiac Diagnostics:** Resting ECG Results, Chest Pain Type, Exercise Induced Angina, ST Depression (Oldpeak), ST Segment Slope
